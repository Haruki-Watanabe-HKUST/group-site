Admissions

We are actively recruiting PhD and MPhil students interested in theoretical condensed matter physics, especially in topics related to symmetry, topology, and the mathematical structure of quantum many-body systems.

Prospective students are warmly encouraged to contact Prof. Watanabe at hwatanabe@ust.hk before applying.

Scholarships and tuition

Full-time MPhil and PhD students are eligible for a Postgraduate Studentship (PGS). The basic rate for the 2026/27 academic year is HK$229,620 per year (HK$19,135 per month), according to the Department of Physics scholarships page. This is the amount before tuition, hall fees, and other charges are settled. For application procedures, deadlines, and scholarship and fee details, please consult the HKUST Postgraduate Admissions page.

The Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS) is a competitive fellowship for PhD applicants. Please see the official page for the latest stipend, tuition waiver and other support, eligibility, and application deadlines.

Tuition for 2026/27 is HK$47,000 per year. Tuition waivers and additional financial support schemes are available, and some students in our group have been awarded such support.

Living costs

Housing costs vary with the hall, room type, location, and living arrangements. The following official figures provide a guide to monthly costs per person.

Single room in postgraduate housingHK$5,394–5,785 per person per month (2026/27)
Shared double room in postgraduate housingHK$3,286–3,441 per person per month (2026/27)
Off-campus rentHK$4,500–18,000 per person per month, utilities excluded (2025/26 estimate, updated April 2026)

Sources: HKUST postgraduate hall charges and the Off-Campus Housing budget guide.

The official off-campus budget estimates HK$11,600–25,100 per person per month for 2025/26 (updated April 2026), including rent, utilities, food, local transport, and personal miscellaneous expenses. Tuition and other university charges are additional. Actual expenses depend on your accommodation and lifestyle.
