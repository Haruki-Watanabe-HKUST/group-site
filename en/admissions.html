Admissions

We are actively recruiting PhD and MPhil students interested in theoretical condensed matter physics, especially in topics related to symmetry, topology, and the mathematical structure of quantum many-body systems.

All admitted postgraduate students at HKUST receive a stipend. For application procedures, deadlines, and details about scholarships and fees, please consult the HKUST Postgraduate Admissions page.

Prospective students are warmly encouraged to contact Prof. Watanabe at hwatanabe@ust.hk before applying.
