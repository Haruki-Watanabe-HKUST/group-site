Getting from the Airport to Our Office

This photo guide explains how to reach the Watanabe Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) from Hong Kong International Airport. Our office is Room 4453, 4/F, Academic Building.

Taking Uber from the airport

After clearing customs and entering Arrivals Hall B, head to Car Park 1 through the exit beneath the blue “Car Park 1” sign behind FINEPRINT. Request your Uber once you reach the car park, and meet the driver at the pickup point shown in the app.

Reference: Uber's Hong Kong International Airport pickup guide.

Getting from the airport to the city

The Airport Express (機場快綫) is a fast, reliable option. The journey from Airport Station to the terminus at Hong Kong Station takes about 24 minutes, with trains approximately every 10 minutes. The first trains depart at 5:54 from the airport and 5:50 from Hong Kong Station; the last trains depart at 0:48 in both directions.

Airport to HKUST via Hong Kong Station and North Point

Follow the route below to reach HKUST from the airport. The approximate fare from Airport Station to Hang Hau Station is HK$120 when you use the same Octopus and enter the MTR within one hour of exiting the Airport Express at Hong Kong Station.

  1. Airport Station → Hong Kong Station: take the Airport Express (機場快綫) towards Hong Kong and get off at the terminus.
  2. Hong Kong Station → Central Station: walk through the connecting passage inside the station complex to the Island Line platforms at Central.
  3. Central Station → North Point Station: take the Island Line (港島綫) towards Chai Wan (柴灣).
  4. North Point Station → Hang Hau Station: after getting off, walk straight across to the opposite side of the same platform and change to the Tseung Kwan O Line (將軍澳綫) towards Po Lam (寶琳).
  5. Hang Hau Station → HKUST: use Exit B1 to reach the bus terminal and take minibus 11M to HKUST North Entrance. See the photo guide from the station to the minibus stop below.

Using Octopus on the Airport Express: you do not need to tap your card when boarding at Airport Station. There are no entry gates at Airport Station, so simply board the train. After getting off at Hong Kong Station, tap your Octopus at the exit gates to pay the fare. Visitors often look for a place to tap before boarding, but no tap is needed there.

These instructions apply to Airport Express journeys from the airport towards the city. For the subsequent regular MTR journey, tap your Octopus when entering Central Station and again when exiting Hang Hau Station.

Hang Hau Station to HKUST's main entrance (minibus 11M)

The fare for minibus 11M from Hang Hau to HKUST is HK$6.5.

Getting to our office (Academic Building, Room 4453)

Our office is Room 4453 on the 4th floor of the Academic Building (學術大樓) at HKUST. From the main entrance, follow these steps.

The 4th floor is the Physics Department floor. Follow the room-number signs along the corridor to Room 4453.

Getting to IAS

The HKUST Jockey Club Institute for Advanced Study (IAS／賽馬會高等研究院) is in the Lo Ka Chung Building (盧家驄薈萃樓) on the HKUST campus. Follow the same route as for our office from the main entrance, past Red Bird and into the Academic Building.
